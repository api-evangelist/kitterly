# Kitterly

> Kitterly, Inc. was a direct-to-consumer craft commerce startup selling knitting and
> crochet kits, yarn, needles and notions, plus a monthly Kit Club subscription that
> shipped a pattern with the yarn needed to complete it. Backed by 500 Global, Manatt
> Ventures and the Jordan x 2080 accelerator, the company was later consolidated under
> the Cotton Cuts brand — a quilting fabric shop selling designer die-cut and pre-cut
> fabric, threads, patterns and notions along with quilting memberships and the Puzzle
> Mystery Quilt program. kitterly.com now redirects to cottoncuts.com.
